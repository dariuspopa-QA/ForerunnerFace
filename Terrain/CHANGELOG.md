# Changelog

## Unreleased

- A data slot in the on-watch editor: the line under the time (default the sunset / sunrise
  line) can show Body Battery, Steps, Heart rate, Stress, Calories, Floors, Intensity minutes or
  Battery instead. The ridge, snow, flag and sun still follow heart rate, stress, steps and the sun.

## 1.0.0 (15 Oct 2026)

Store text ("What's new", also in `store/description.txt`):

> • First release, on 66 round Garmin watches (AMOLED and MIP).

Details:

- Every round watch on API 5.0+ (66 products, 218-466 px), like ApexFace: fonts for nine screen
  sizes, the on-watch editor wherever the firmware has it.
- MIP watches: a palette for 64 colours (black sky, navy far range, grey rock; the blue-greys
  would round to teal), no Always-On (the full face stays on).
- Store listing: `store/description.txt`, images in `store/` (`FaceCore/tools/store-shots.ps1`).
