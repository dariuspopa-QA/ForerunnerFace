APP NAME
Terrain

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The last 24 hours of your heart rate as a mountain ridge, with the sun on its way.

DESCRIPTION
Terrain draws your day as a landscape. The ridge is your heart rate over the last 24 hours, one peak for every hour, with now at the right end. Your workout is the summit. Snow covers the peaks down to a line set by your stress, a flag on the ridge shows how far you are to your step goal, and the sun crosses the sky from sunrise to sunset (the moon at night).

What's on the face
• Mountain ridge: the highest heart rate of each of the last 24 hours, with the current reading at the end
• Snow line: lower when you're stressed, high when you're calm
• Summit flag: your progress to the step goal, with the percentage
• Sun on a dotted arc from sunrise to sunset; a crescent moon at night
• Thin, large time, the date, and the next sunset or sunrise
• Weekday and date: SAT 10 OCT, or DD-MM-YYYY / MM-DD-YYYY as your watch shows it
• 12-hour or 24-hour time, as set on your watch

Colours
Flag: Ember, Mint, Sky or Amber. On MIP watches the night sky and mountains use colours made for their 64-colour screens.

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows the ridge as a fine line and a thin time. It lights very few pixels and moves slightly every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on.

Customise
• On your phone: open Terrain in the Connect IQ Store app and change the flag colour and date format.
• On the watch (where the firmware has the watch face editor): from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to pick the flag colour.

Notes
• Terrain keeps the hourly heart rate on the watch, so the ridge stays complete even on watches that hold less than 24 hours of history. Missing hours are filled in between their neighbours.
• Sunrise and sunset come from your watch's weather data or last GPS position.
• Your data stays on the watch. Terrain doesn't send anything anywhere.
