APP NAME
Minimal Face

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The time, one ring and two fields. Nothing else unless you want it.

DESCRIPTION
Minimal Face keeps it simple: a big, clean time, a thin ring around the edge for your steps or battery, and two fields underneath. Pick an accent colour and a time weight and you're done.

What's on the face
• Big time in Light, Regular or Bold, 12-hour or 24-hour (or follow the watch)
• Weekday and date: MON 28 SEP, DD-MM-YYYY or MM-DD-YYYY
• Ring around the edge: steps to your step goal, watch battery, or off
• Left and right field: Heart rate, Battery, Steps or Calories
• Notification count, alarm count and do-not-disturb icon (each can be turned off)

Colours and backgrounds
Accent: Lime, Ice blue, Orange, Pink or White. Background: none, Orbits, Waves, Halftone dots, Blobs, Topographic or Rays, in subtle, medium or bold intensity, tinted with the accent or grey.

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows a dimmed time and (if you want) the date. It moves slightly every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on.

Customise
• On your phone: open Minimal Face in the Connect IQ Store app and change its settings: accent, background, time weight, ring, fields, clock and date format, status icons.
• On the watch (where the firmware has the watch face editor): from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to pick the background, accent colour and ring.
• Whatever you changed last, on the phone or on the watch, is what the face shows.

Notes
• Any value that isn't available on your watch is shown as "--".
• Your data stays on the watch. Minimal Face doesn't send anything anywhere.
