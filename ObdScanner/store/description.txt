APP NAME
OBD Scanner

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Your watch as a diagnostic tester: live PIDs, a heart-rate graph and a fault code.

DESCRIPTION
OBD Scanner is part of Shop Floor, a family of watch faces for people who work on cars. It looks like the screen of a scan tool plugged into your own diagnostic port: one-colour LCD, a live PID table and a trouble code at the bottom.

What's on the face
• Check-engine lamp and "OBD-II · LIVE"
• Time with seconds, and the date
• PID table: HEART.RATE, STEP.COUNT, BATT.VOLT (the watch battery as a car battery's voltage, 11.8-12.7 V) and STRESS.LOAD
• Bar graph of your heart rate over the last 24 hours (the highest reading of each hour)
• Fault code: P0562 SYSTEM VOLTAGE LOW below 15 % battery, P0217 when your stress runs over 75, and otherwise a rotating joke code such as P1010 10MM SOCKET NOT FOUND
• Weekday and date: SAT 11 OCT, or DD-MM-YYYY / MM-DD-YYYY as your watch shows it
• 12-hour or 24-hour time, as set on your watch

Colours
Screen: Amber, Green, Cyan or Red.

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows the lamp, a dim time and the fault code. It lights very few pixels and moves slightly every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on; the seconds show while the watch is awake.

Customise
• On your phone: open OBD Scanner in the Connect IQ Store app and change the screen colour and date format.
• On the watch (where the firmware has the watch face editor): from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to pick the screen colour, and under Data what each of the four PID rows shows (heart rate, steps, battery and stress unless you pick other readings).

Notes
• OBD Scanner keeps the hourly heart rate on the watch, so the graph stays complete on watches that hold less history.
• Any value that isn't available is shown as "--".
• Your data stays on the watch. OBD Scanner doesn't connect to a car or send anything anywhere.
