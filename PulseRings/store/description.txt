APP NAME
Pulse Rings

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The time inside two rings: steps to your goal and Body Battery, plus two fields of your choice.

DESCRIPTION
Pulse Rings wraps the time in two rings. The outer ring fills with your steps towards your step goal; the inner ring shows your Body Battery, coloured red, orange, yellow or green like Garmin's own. Below the time, two fields of your choice and the next sunrise or sunset.

What's on the face
• Outer ring: step progress in your accent colour
• Inner ring: Body Battery level, coloured by Garmin's convention
• Big time in Light, Regular or Bold
• Weekday and date: MON 28 SEP, DD-MM-YYYY or MM-DD-YYYY
• Two fields, each one of: Heart rate, Stress, Recovery time, Calories, Watch battery, Body Battery or Steps
• Sunrise and sunset times
• Notification count, alarm count and do-not-disturb icon (each can be turned off)

Colours and backgrounds
Accent: Lime, Ice blue, Orange, Pink or White. Background: none, Orbits, Waves, Halftone dots, Blobs, Topographic or Rays, in subtle, medium or bold intensity, tinted with the accent or grey.

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows a dimmed time with hour ticks and (if you want) the date. It moves slightly every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on.

Customise
• On your phone: open Pulse Rings in the Connect IQ Store app and change its settings: accent, background, time weight, fields, date format, sunrise / sunset and status icons.
• On the watch (where the firmware has the watch face editor): from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to pick the background, accent colour and the two fields.
• Whatever you changed last, on the phone or on the watch, is what the face shows.

Notes
• Sunrise and sunset come from your watch's last GPS position.
• Any value that isn't available on your watch is shown as "--".
• Your data stays on the watch. Pulse Rings doesn't send anything anywhere.
