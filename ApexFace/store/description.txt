APP NAME
APEX

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A bold, minimal face: huge time, one accent colour, one arc, three data fields.

DESCRIPTION
APEX is a clean, high-contrast watch face for round Garmin watches, AMOLED and MIP. Pure black ground, near-white type and a single accent colour. Nothing more.

Big time, one font
Hours in bold, minutes in regular weight of the same condensed type. The difference is weight, not colour, so the time reads at a glance.

What's on the face
• A 270° arc around the dial, with its value at 6 o'clock: Body Battery, Step goal, Battery, Stress or Intensity minutes (or turn it off)
• Three data fields under the time, each one your choice
• A single accent tick that sweeps the rim for seconds
• Weekday and date in your watch's style (DD-MM-YYYY or MM-DD-YYYY), or MON 28 SEP
• 12-hour or 24-hour time, as set on your watch, with or without a colon

Data fields
Heart rate, Steps, Distance, Calories, Floors, Stress, Body Battery, Recovery time, Sunrise, Sunset, Altitude, Temperature, Battery, Notifications, Seconds, Intensity minutes, Pulse Ox, Respiration, Sleep score (where your watch has it), VO2 max (run or bike), Pressure, Weekly run distance, Solar intensity (solar watches), or Empty. Leave a slot empty and the row re-centres.
The same list is in the on-watch editor and in the Connect IQ app.

Colours
• Accent: Volt, Ember, Glacier, Signal, Amber, Mint, Cobalt, Violet, Scarlet, Ghost
• Time digits: Chalk, Bone, Ice, Silver, Same as accent, or any accent hue
• Data values and labels: three tones each

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows only the date, a thin time and the 12 hour marks. It lights few pixels and moves the content every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on, seconds included.

Night mode
A red palette that's easy on your eyes in the dark: Off, On, or Auto (between the sleep and wake times in your user profile). Watches with Garmin's Red Shift tint the face red on their own, too.

Customise
• On the watch: from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to change the data and colours.
• On your phone: open APEX in the Connect IQ Store app and change its settings, including Night mode, the date format and the colon between hours and minutes.

Notes
• Distance, altitude and temperature follow your watch's unit settings.
• Sunrise, sunset and temperature come from your watch's weather data. They show "--" until weather is available (phone connected).
• Any value that isn't available is shown as "--".
• Your data stays on the watch. APEX doesn't send anything anywhere.

WHAT'S NEW (version 1.0.4)
• New look: the face uses Roboto again, as in the first version, with lighter hours.
• Larger date, data fields and arc value; the time keeps its size.
• New option: a colon between hours and minutes. Turn on "Colon between hours and minutes" in the face's settings in the Connect IQ app, or pick the "APEX with colon" style in the on-watch editor.
• More data fields in the Connect IQ app, the same as on the watch: Intensity minutes, Pulse Ox, Respiration, Sleep score, VO2 max (run and bike), Pressure, Weekly run distance and Solar intensity.
• On-watch editor: the data you scroll to is shown right away, and the left field stays in place while you edit it.
