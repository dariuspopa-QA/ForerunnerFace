APP NAME
Lug Star

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Wrench hands over a five-lug hub: your step goal torques the wheel nuts in a star.

DESCRIPTION
Lug Star is part of Shop Floor, a family of watch faces for people who work on cars. The hands are combination wrenches turning over a five-lug wheel hub. The nuts are numbered in the star pattern you tighten them in, and your steps do the torquing: every fifth of your step goal tightens the next nut and draws its line of the star. At 100 % the star closes.

What's on the face
• Hour and minute hands shaped like combination wrenches, and a second hand in the accent colour
• Five lug nuts numbered in tightening order (1-3-5-2-4), lit as you reach each fifth of your step goal
• The star lines between the tightened nuts, dashed ahead
• TORQUE SEQUENCE: nuts done out of 5 and today's steps (or another reading of your choice, set on the watch)
• Minute track with hour marks, and the date at the top: SAT 11 OCT, or DD-MM-YYYY / MM-DD-YYYY as your watch shows it

Colours
Accent: Hi-Vis Yellow, Safety Orange, Coolant Blue or Redline.

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows the nut outlines (tightened ones in the accent) and thin hour and minute hands. It lights very few pixels and moves slightly every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on; the second hand shows while the watch is awake.

Customise
• On your phone: open Lug Star in the Connect IQ Store app and change the accent colour and date format.
• On the watch (where the firmware has the watch face editor): from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to pick the accent colour, and under Data the reading after the nut count (steps unless you pick Body Battery, steps, heart rate, stress, calories, floors, intensity minutes or battery).

Notes
• Without a step goal on the watch, Lug Star counts to 10,000 steps.
• Your data stays on the watch. Lug Star doesn't send anything anywhere.
