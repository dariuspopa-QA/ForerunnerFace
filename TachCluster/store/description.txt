APP NAME
Tach Cluster

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A rev counter for your wrist: the needle shows the hour, the gear shows the day.

DESCRIPTION
Tach Cluster is part of Shop Floor, a family of watch faces for people who work on cars. It reads like an instrument cluster: a rev counter from 0 to 12 whose needle is the hour hand, with the last two hours in the red zone, and an LCD for the exact time.

What's on the face
• Rev counter 0-12 with quarter-hour ticks that light up to the needle; 10-12 is the red zone
• The needle in your accent colour shows the time of day like an hour hand
• Gear indicator: the weekday (P on Sunday, 1-6 Monday to Saturday)
• LCD: the time, and today's steps as the ODO reading
• Fuel gauge: the watch battery, E to F
• Temperature gauge: your heart rate, C to H (it runs hot in a workout)
• 12-hour or 24-hour time on the LCD, as set on your watch

Colours
Needle: Safety Orange, Hi-Vis Yellow, Coolant Blue or Redline.

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows the hour ticks dimmed, a thin needle and the LCD time. It lights very few pixels and moves slightly every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on.

Customise
• On your phone: open Tach Cluster in the Connect IQ Store app and change the needle colour.
• On the watch (where the firmware has the watch face editor): from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to pick the needle colour, and under Data what the ODO line and the caption above the hub show.

Notes

• Your data stays on the watch. Tach Cluster doesn't send anything anywhere.
