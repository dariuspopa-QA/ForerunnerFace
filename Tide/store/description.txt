APP NAME
Tide

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Your Body Battery as water: the screen fills up, the waves follow your stress.

DESCRIPTION
Tide turns your energy into water. The screen fills to your Body Battery level, so a full tank means a full screen and an empty one leaves just a puddle at the bottom. The time stands huge in the middle; where the water covers the digits they turn dark.

What's on the face
• Water level: your Body Battery, with the number at the surface
• Waves: calm when you're relaxed, rougher as your stress rises
• A second wave drifts behind, faster when your heart rate is higher
• Bubbles: one for every 1,000 steps (up to nine)
• Huge hours over minutes, light above the water and dark below it
• Weekday and date at the top: SAT 10 OCT, or DD-MM-YYYY / MM-DD-YYYY as your watch shows it
• Today's steps at the bottom (or another reading of your choice, set on the watch)
• 12-hour or 24-hour time, as set on your watch

Colours
Water: Lagoon, Coral, Lime or Iris.

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows only the water line and thin digits. It lights very few pixels and moves slightly every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on.

Customise
• On your phone: open Tide in the Connect IQ Store app and change the water colour and date format.
• On the watch (where the firmware has the watch face editor): from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to pick the water colour, and under Data what the number at the surface and the line at the bottom show (Body Battery, steps, heart rate, stress, calories, floors, intensity minutes or battery).

Notes
• Body Battery and stress need a watch that measures them. Without a reading the water sits at half.
• Your data stays on the watch. Tide doesn't send anything anywhere.
