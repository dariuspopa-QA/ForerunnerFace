APP NAME
Flat Rate

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A shift clock for the shop: time on the clock, booked hours, time left, next stop.

DESCRIPTION
Flat Rate is part of Shop Floor, a family of watch faces for people who work on cars. It's a shift clock: a ring of 100 segments fills as your shift goes by, the boxes under the time show how long you've been on the clock and how much is left, and a hazard-stripe band names the next stop.

What's on the face
• Shift ring: fills from the start of your shift to its end; the last tenth turns red
• Repair order and bay number on the top line (each hidden when set to 0), and your own job line
• Big time
• ON CLOCK: time since the shift started; BOOK: the shift length in hours; LEFT: time to the end, in the accent colour
• Hazard band with the next stop: START before the shift, then BREAK and END with their times, and SHIFT OVER when you're done
• Weekday, date and heart rate at the bottom; the date as SAT 11 OCT, or DD-MM-YYYY / MM-DD-YYYY as your watch shows it
• 12-hour or 24-hour time, as set on your watch

Settings
• Shift start (05:00-11:00), shift length (4-12 hours), break time (or no break)
• Repair order number, bay number and the job line text (up to 22 characters)
• Accent: Hi-Vis, Yellow, Safety Orange or Coolant Blue
• Date format

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows the shift as a thin arc, the next stop and thin digits. It lights very few pixels and moves slightly every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on.

Customise
• On your phone: open Flat Rate in the Connect IQ Store app and set your shift, break, repair order, bay, job line, accent colour and date format.
• On the watch (where the firmware has the watch face editor): from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to pick the accent colour.
