APP NAME
Goal Bars

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Big two-tone time and four goal bars: steps, floors, Body Battery, active minutes and more.

DESCRIPTION
Goal Bars shows how far you are towards each of your daily goals as one tall bar, next to the time in big two-tone digits. Every metric has its own colour, so you know which bar is which at a glance, and pace markers show where you should be by now.

What's on the face
• Big time: hours over minutes (Stacked) or hours:minutes centred (Classic), in Bold or Regular
• Four goal bars, each one any of: Steps, Floors climbed, Active minutes (week), Move alert, Body Battery, Stress or Active calories (goal of 300, 500, 750 or 1000 kcal)
• Pace markers on the bars: where you should be at this time of day
• Weekday and date: MON 28 SEP, DD-MM-YYYY or MM-DD-YYYY
• Heart rate at the bottom, watch battery as an arc on the left bezel
• Notification count, alarm count and do-not-disturb icon (each can be turned off)
• 12-hour or 24-hour time, or follow the watch

Colours and backgrounds
Accent: Lime, Ice blue, Orange, Pink or White. Background: none, Orbits, Waves, Halftone dots, Blobs, Topographic, Rays, Aurora or Deep Space, in subtle, medium or bold intensity, tinted with the accent or grey.

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows a grey, slightly smaller time with hour ticks and (if you want) the date. It moves slightly every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on.

Customise
• On your phone: open Goal Bars in the Connect IQ Store app and change its settings: layout, accent, background, bars, calories goal, pace markers, clock and date format, status icons.
• On the watch (where the firmware has the watch face editor): from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to pick the background, accent colour, bars and bottom field.
• Whatever you changed last, on the phone or on the watch, is what the face shows.

Notes
• Goals come from your Garmin Connect profile. Garmin has no calorie goal, so you pick one in the settings.
• Any value that isn't available on your watch is shown as "--".
• Your data stays on the watch. Goal Bars doesn't send anything anywhere.
