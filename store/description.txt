APP NAME
APEX

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A bold, minimal face: huge time, one accent colour, one arc, three data fields.

DESCRIPTION
APEX is a clean, high-contrast watch face for round Garmin watches, AMOLED and MIP. Pure black ground, near-white type and a single accent colour. Nothing more.

Big time, one font
Hours in the heaviest weight, minutes in the thinnest weight of the same condensed type. The difference is weight, not colour, so the time reads at a glance.

What's on the face
• A 270° arc around the dial, with its value at 6 o'clock: Body Battery, Step goal, Battery, Stress or Intensity minutes (or turn it off)
• Three data fields under the time, each one your choice
• A single accent tick that sweeps the rim for seconds
• Weekday and date

Data fields
Heart rate, Steps, Distance, Calories, Floors, Stress, Body Battery, Recovery time, Sunrise, Sunset, Altitude, Temperature, Battery, Notifications, Seconds, or Empty. Leave a slot empty and the row re-centres.
In the on-watch editor you can also pick other system data, such as Pulse Ox, Respiration, Sleep score (where your watch has it), VO2 max, Pressure or Weekly run distance.

Colours
• Accent: Volt, Ember, Glacier, Signal, Amber, Mint, Cobalt, Violet, Scarlet, Ghost
• Time digits: Chalk, Bone, Ice, Silver, Same as accent, or any accent hue
• Data values and labels: three tones each

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows only the date, a thin time and the 12 hour marks. It lights few pixels and moves the content every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on, seconds included.

Night mode
A red palette that's easy on your eyes in the dark: Off, On, or Auto (between the sleep and wake times in your user profile). Watches with Garmin's Red Shift tint the face red on their own, too.

Customise
• On the watch: from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to change the data and colours.
• On your phone: open APEX in the Connect IQ Store app and change its settings, including Night mode.

Notes
• Distance, altitude and temperature follow your watch's unit settings.
• Sunrise, sunset and temperature come from your watch's weather data. They show "--" until weather is available (phone connected).
• Any value that isn't available is shown as "--".
• Your data stays on the watch. APEX doesn't send anything anywhere.
