APP NAME
Socket Set

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A watch face for mechanics: socket-end hour markers, a ratchet ring, and no 10 mm.

DESCRIPTION
Socket Set is part of Shop Floor, a family of watch faces for people who work on cars. The hour markers are socket ends from 8 to 19 mm (the hour plus 7), and the current hour's socket lights up. The 10 mm is missing, as always. A 72-tooth ratchet ring clicks round with the minutes.

What's on the face
• Twelve socket-end hour markers, 8-19 mm, the current one lit in your accent colour; the 10 mm is a dashed outline marked "10?"
• Ratchet ring: 72 teeth that fill up over the hour
• Big time with the seconds in the accent colour
• HR: your heart rate
• BATT: the watch battery as a car battery's resting voltage (11.8 V empty, 12.7 V full)
• ODO: today's steps
• Weekday and date: SAT 11 OCT, or DD-MM-YYYY / MM-DD-YYYY as your watch shows it
• 12-hour or 24-hour time, as set on your watch

Colours
Accent: Safety Orange, Hi-Vis Yellow, Coolant Blue or Redline.

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows the socket outlines (the current hour in the accent) and thin digits. It lights very few pixels and moves slightly every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on; the seconds show while the watch is awake.

Customise
• On your phone: open Socket Set in the Connect IQ Store app and change the accent colour and date format.
• On the watch (where the firmware has the watch face editor): from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to pick the accent colour, and under Data what the three readouts under the time show (heart rate, battery and ODO steps unless you pick other readings).

Notes
• Any value that isn't available is shown as "--".
• Your data stays on the watch. Socket Set doesn't send anything anywhere.
