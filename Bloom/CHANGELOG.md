# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (11 Oct 2026)

Store text ("What's new", also in `store/description.txt`):

> • First release: the five-lobed bloom, eight colours plus your own hex colour, a choice of highlight colour for the lowest value, and an always-on mode.

Details:

- First release on fr265, fr265s, fr965 and fr970 (AMOLED, API 5.0+).
- Colours are editable. `Palette` grows from Rose / Sage / Sky to eight presets (adds Coral,
  Gold, Lilac, Aqua, Pearl; mid / core layers are the outer colour at 65 % / 30 % over
  white). New `CustomColor` (hex text, phone only, overrides the palette) and `Highlight`
  (colour of the lowest value, default orange `0xFF8A3D`, seven choices).
- fr970 on-watch editor: eight styles (= palettes) and seven accent colours (= highlight).
- Phone settings vs. the editor: whichever was changed last wins (`ColorsFromEditor` in
  `Application.Storage`). Before, the editor's default style always overrode the phone's
  Palette setting on the fr970.
- Store images: `store/` (icon, cover, 7 screenshots, 5 showcase shots, hero with and
  without text), generated by `store/tools/` from simulator renders.
