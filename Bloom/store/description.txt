APP NAME
Bloom

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Your day in one soft shape: energy, steps, sleep, calm and recovery around the time.

DESCRIPTION
Bloom turns your day's balance into a single five-lobed shape for round Garmin watches, AMOLED and MIP. Each lobe swells with one of five readings, so a full, round bloom means a good day. A thin, dented one shows at a glance what needs attention. The time sits in the middle of the shape.

The five lobes (clockwise from the top)
• ENERGY: Body Battery
• STEPS: steps towards your daily goal
• SLEEP: last night's sleep score
• CALM: the opposite of your stress level
• RECOVERY: how far you are through your recovery time (fully recovered = full lobe)

Each value is printed next to its lobe. The lowest one is highlighted, so you know what to work on.

What's on the face
• The bloom: three nested layers of one colour, redrawn as your readings change
• Large time and the date in the core
• Your heart rate at the bottom of the dial, or another reading of your choice (set on the watch)
• Weekday and date in your watch's style (DD-MM-YYYY or MM-DD-YYYY), or the short SAT 10
• 12-hour or 24-hour time, as set on your watch

Colours
• Bloom colour: Rose, Sage, Sky, Coral, Gold, Lilac, Aqua or Pearl
• Or your own: enter any hex colour (for example 7A5CFF) and Bloom works out the lighter layers for you
• Lowest value colour: Orange, Red, Yellow, Lime, Cyan, Magenta or White

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows only the outline of the bloom and a thin time. It lights very few pixels and shifts slightly every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on.

Customise
• On your phone: open Bloom in the Connect IQ Store app and change its settings: bloom colour, custom colour, lowest value colour and date format.
• On the watch (where the firmware has the watch face editor): from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to pick the bloom colour (Style), the highlight colour (Accent), and under Data the reading at the bottom of the dial (heart rate unless you pick Body Battery, steps, heart rate, stress, calories, floors, intensity minutes or battery).
• Whatever you changed last, on the phone or on the watch, is what the face shows.

Notes
• Sleep score needs a watch that publishes it to watch faces. Anywhere a value isn't available, it shows "--" and its lobe sits at half size.
• Recovery counts down from 72 hours: 0 h left is a full lobe, 72 h or more is empty.
• Your data stays on the watch. Bloom doesn't send anything anywhere.
