APP NAME
Bearing

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A minimalist compass dial with a thin time, your battery and your steps.

DESCRIPTION
Bearing is a quiet, compass-style face. A fine 60-tick bezel with a north marker frames a large, thin time, the minutes in your accent colour. Battery sits above, today's steps below. Nothing else.

What's on the face
• Compass bezel: 60 ticks, accent marks every five minutes, long cardinal marks and a north triangle
• Thin, large time: hours in white, minutes in the accent colour
• Battery: icon filled to the charge, with the percentage
• Steps today
• Both lines can show another reading of your choice, set on the watch
• 12-hour or 24-hour time, as set on your watch

Colours
Accent: Ice, Mint, Amber or Ember. On MIP watches, Ice shows as a sky blue made for their 64-colour screens.

Always-on mode
On AMOLED watches, always-on mode shows the hour marks, the north marker and a dimmed time. It lights very few pixels and moves slightly every minute to protect the screen. On MIP watches the full face stays on.

Customise
• On your phone: open Bearing in the Connect IQ Store app and pick the accent colour.
• On the watch (where the firmware has the watch face editor): from the watch face, hold the menu button, select Watch Face, then START > Customize to pick the accent colour, and under Data what the lines above and below the time show (battery and steps unless you pick Body Battery, heart rate, stress, sunset, calories, floors or intensity minutes).

Notes
• The bezel is a compass look; the face doesn't show a live heading.
• Your data stays on the watch. Bearing doesn't send anything anywhere.
